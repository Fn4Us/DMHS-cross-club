      How to play

      • Click any square, or a clue, to start there.
      • Click a square a second time to switch between Across and Down.
      • Type letters to fill the grid — the site jumps to the next square automatically.
      • Arrow keys move around the grid. Tab / Shift+Tab jump to the next / previous clue. Space switches direction. Backspace deletes and steps back.
      • Pencil marks new letters as unconfirmed guesses instead of ink.
      • Auto-check flags wrong letters the moment you type them.
      • Check flags mistakes without giving away the answer. Reveal fills in the correct letter(s).
      • A small red corner mark means a square was wrong at some point; a small dot means it was revealed.
